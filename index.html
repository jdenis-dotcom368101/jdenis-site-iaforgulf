Does the EU AI Act reach your systems?

The AI Act follows the output, not the head office. If a system you build or operate produces results that are used inside the European Union, you are in scope — wherever your company is registered.

The deadlines also moved this summer: Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, the Digital Omnibus in force since 27 July 2026, pushed Annex III high-risk back to 2 December 2027 and Annex I to 2 August 2028, while the Article 50 transparency duties have applied since 2 August 2026. Most timelines still circulating online show the old calendar.

Do you operate on the ground in the UAE?

Yes. Engagements are delivered on-site in Dubai and across the GCC, and remotely, in English or French. I work from Europe and travel for diagnostics, training and key milestones.

You get a senior, independent practitioner directly — not a rotating junior team — plus European governance standards your stakeholders increasingly expect. No licences to resell and no vendor lock-in: recommendations are arbitrated on your ROI alone.

Yes. I design the architecture and can deliver the build, notably through NOIA NOGAIN, the custom AI-agent practice I co-founded, with sovereign hosting and reversibility built in.
